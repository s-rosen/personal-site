I am a linguist specializing in phonetics, phonology, and prosody. Since 2023 I have worked on speech and voice AI evaluation at ServiceNow. Senior ML Data Linguist

Feb 2026 – Present
ServiceNow, LUNAR team (Linguistic Unit for NLU Analysis & Research)
  • Co-designed a 10-dimension annotation schema that breaks TTS “naturalness” into word-level, prosodic, and paralinguistic dimensions, and built an 860-utterance benchmark labeled by trained linguist raters (EMNLP 2026, co-first author).
  • Compared eight automated speech evaluators (four MOS predictors, four audio-LLM judges) against human labels under four prompting conditions, MOS predictors mostly tracked acoustic signal quality, and audio-LLM judges caught some error types depending on the prompt. Neither reliably detected the full range of linguistic errors.
  • Designed and ran a multilingual TTS vendor comparison (Cartesia vs. ElevenLabs). Wrote test sentences drawn from ServiceNow case language and text normalization edge cases (symbols, math, units, URLs), defined a rubric pairing holistic 1–5 ratings with per-category head-to-head judgments, and adapted it for Japanese with a native-speaker linguist.
  • Built a rating framework for AI voice agent conversations covering speech fluency, tool calling, task completion, and helpfulness, and wrote its annotation guidelines.
  • Ran the labeling efforts that produced human benchmarks for customer-facing voice fluency judges: wrote the guidelines, onboarded labelers, and presented results across agents, separating ASR-driven failures (such as repeated confirmations) from conversation-flow and voice-style problems.
  • Leading success labeling for document question answering (DocQnA) and building the matching LLM judge.
  • Built and maintain the internal labeling tools used on these projects, and the team’s project-tracking app.
